Spoken points come out as a numbered list.

## Download

| | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `Yappaflow_0.9.9_x64-setup.exe` |

Check your download against `SHA256SUMS`. If you have 0.9.2 or newer, you don't need to download anything: Yappaflow offers this update by itself. Installing over an older version keeps your settings, history and keys.

## Improved

- **Numbered points, however you say them.** Saying "That's point one. Point two, ..." after a point, not only before it, now turns each point into its own numbered paragraph, and the words "point one", "point two" are left out of the text.
