Check your download against `SHA256SUMS`. If you have 0.9.2 or newer, you don't need to download anything: Yappaflow offers this update by itself. Installing over an older version keeps your settings, history and keys.

## New

- **Learns names you correct.** Fix the spelling of a name right after a dictation (say "Shaheed" to "Shahid") and the pill says "Adding Shahid to dictionary". A bar counts down 5 seconds, then the name is added. Press **Undo** to stop it. Only names and unusual words (with a capital or a digit) are learned. Turn it off with **Learn from my corrections** on the Dictionary page. This replaces the old "corrected twice" prompt.
- **Changed your mind after cancelling?** Pressing Esc while recording or transcribing now shows "Dictation cancelled" with a **Rerun** button and a 2.5-second countdown. Press Rerun to transcribe and paste that recording after all.

## Fixed

- **No more stray space before your text.** In chat apps like Discord, a dictation into an empty message box started with a space. Yappaflow now adds a joining space only when the text before the cursor is really in that box.
