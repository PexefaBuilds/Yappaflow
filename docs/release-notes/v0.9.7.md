## New

- **Learns names you correct.** Fix the spelling of a name right after a dictation (say "Shaheed" to "Shahid") and the pill says "Adding Shahid to dictionary". A bar counts down 5 seconds, then the name is added. Press **Undo** to stop it. Only names and unusual words (with a capital or a digit) are learned. Turn it off with **Learn from my corrections** on the Dictionary page. This replaces the old "corrected twice" prompt.
- **Cancelling shows "Dictation cancelled".** Pressing Esc while recording now says so on the pill, instead of the pill just vanishing.
