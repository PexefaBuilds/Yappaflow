Check your download against `SHA256SUMS`. If you have 0.9.2 or newer, you don't need to download anything: Yappaflow offers this update by itself. Installing over an older version keeps your settings, history and keys.

## New

- **Real OpenAI spend (optional).** In History, open **Real OpenAI spend (optional)** under the cost estimate and paste an OpenAI Admin key (`sk-admin-…`, read-only is enough). Yappaflow then shows what OpenAI actually billed this month for the project your OpenAI key belongs to, counting every key and device in that project. The admin key is stored in your OS keychain, sent only to OpenAI, and used only when you open History. Without one, nothing changes.
